AI-Augmented Engagement Operating System

From AWP to traceable fieldwork and reviewed workpapers.

AEOS is a file-based operating system for auditors working with AI. It turns an audit work program (AWP) into a controlled route for evidence, testing, workpapers and reporting drafts without losing the audit trail. It is independently developed and under evaluation; it has not been externally validated and is not production-ready.

Purpose, scope and practical benefit.

01 · Purpose

Give the engagement a shared operating structure.

AEOS gives the auditor and AI one visible place for context, source boundaries, procedures, evidence, draft outputs and review decisions.

02 · Scope

Support fieldwork from the audit work program onward.

It covers fieldwork preparation, meetings, evidence handling, assessment of control design and implementation, operating-effectiveness testing where applicable, quality review and reporting drafts.

03 · Benefit

Spend less time rebuilding the process and more time reviewing the evidence.

AEOS prepares structured drafts, keeps source links and open gaps visible, and applies consistent routes and review points across comparable work.

The professional concepts draw on public audit guidance. The workflow stages, labels and workspace structure shown here are AEOS design choices, not an IIA-prescribed methodology.

How it works

Inputs pass through a governed operating core before they become audit outputs.

The components have distinct jobs. The workspace holds the engagement, the task context sets the professional and file boundaries, the governed workflow controls the task, and the auditor authorizes each reliance decision.

Engagement inputs flow through the governed operating core into draft audit work, while human decisions retain reliance and communication authority.

How AEOS supports the auditor

Reduce manual preparation without outsourcing audit judgment.

AEOS takes on repeatable preparation, organization, comparison and drafting tasks. This is intended to improve quality, consistency and efficiency. The auditor still decides what the evidence means and whether the work may move forward.

Practical fieldwork support
For each AWP procedure, AEOS can prepare workpaper scaffolds for design-and-implementation assessment and operating-effectiveness testing, fieldwork plans, walkthrough topics, evidence-request candidates, testing records, progress updates and reporting drafts for auditor review.
Quality
Procedure wording, source identity, evidence, gaps, contradictions, testing and proposed conclusions remain connected. This makes unsupported claims and incomplete work easier to identify before reliance.
Consistency
Comparable work follows the same minimum inputs, workflow stages, templates, stop conditions and review checkpoints, while the auditor retains professional judgment.
Efficiency
AEOS is designed to reduce repeated workspace setup, manual copying, source hunting, formatting and status drafting, leaving more auditor time for evidence sufficiency, exceptions and conclusions.
Visible control
The system leaves missing information unresolved, records the next decision and stops at defined review gates. The auditor approves scope, population, sampling, reliance, conclusions and communication.

What AEOS includes

A readable system of files, workflows and controls.

AEOS runs in Visual Studio Code and Obsidian. There is no separate backend. The product is the configured workspace and the governed routes inside it.

AEOS components, their jobs and their relationships
Component Job Relationship
Engagement workspace Holds context, plans, evidence references, workpapers, reviews and reporting drafts. The shared environment used by every other component.
Task contexts Set the professional perspective and operating boundaries for the selected task. A person selects the context before invoking a workflow.
Governed workflows Define the inputs, sequence, checks, stop conditions and output for a specific audit task. The selected context runs one workflow against the authorized source set.
Workflow launch controls Give the auditor a repeatable way to start each supported route. They call the owning workflow; they do not create a competing process.
Templates and guides Provide readable output structures and operator instructions. Workflows use the templates; auditors use the guides and review the result.
Evidence and review controls Track sources, provenance, draft state, corrections, gaps and human approvals. They sit across every route and prevent draft output from becoming silent authority.
Python checks Validate structure, paths, packaging and other deterministic properties. They support the workflow but do not decide audit evidence or conclusions.

Implementation depth

The hard problems behind governed AI fieldwork.

A fluent draft is the visible part. The harder engineering problem is preserving source authority, evidence state and human decision rights as work moves through the engagement. AEOS is being developed around those transitions.

Current foundation · Flexible approved inputs
Differently structured AWPs can enter through governed interpretation without forcing every engagement into one universal handoff schema.
Current foundation · Visible evidence states
Original source, reviewed working copy, inference, gap, contradiction, test result and draft conclusion remain distinguishable.
Current foundation · Explicit authority transitions
Evidence reliance, sampling, exceptions, conclusions, sign-off and communication remain auditor decisions.
Current foundation · Reusable core, private boundary
Product logic is separated from organization-specific evidence. Native originals remain authoritative, while agents use reviewed, source-linked Markdown working copies.
Active challenge · One authoritative lifecycle
Current hardening is consolidating each stage under one workflow owner so that different launch routes cannot become competing processes.
Active challenge · Controlled trail discovery
The planned inquiry capability must find useful connections inside authorized scope without turning relatedness into permission or evidence reliance.
Active challenge · Tests of behavior, not vocabulary
A control sentence is insufficient if a later instruction contradicts it. Tests must exercise the behavior rather than merely search for the expected wording.
Active challenge · Recovery without skipped gates
Interrupted work must resume without duplicated output, stale state or a bypassed human checkpoint.
Active challenge · Representative acceptance
Clean-environment invocation, end-to-end runs, adversarial scenarios and first-user operation remain open before any production claim.
Maturity boundary
The current foundations are implemented and tested at source level. Broader runtime, operator and representative-engagement validation remains open.

Scope and authority

What AEOS does, what the auditor owns, and where the boundary sits.

AEOS supports
  • AWP intake and workpaper setup
  • Walkthroughs, meetings and evidence requests
  • Design-and-implementation assessment and operating-effectiveness testing
  • Separate review, correction and merge routes
  • Progress, reporting and lessons-review drafts
The auditor owns
  • Scope, criteria and source authorization
  • Population, sampling and exception decisions
  • Evidence sufficiency and reliance
  • Conclusions, sign-off and reporting judgment
  • Every external communication
Outside AEOS
  • Discovery and creation of the audit work program
  • Autonomous approval, sign-off or transmission
  • Enterprise audit scheduling or budget ownership
  • Use of restricted evidence on an unapproved route
  • Final engagement conclusions or observation closure on the auditor's behalf

Common questions

The short answers.

Where does AEOS start?
After the audit work program (AWP) has been reviewed and authorized under the internal audit function's methodology. Missing planning decisions remain open for the auditor.
Does AEOS replace the auditor?
No. It supports preparation, evidence analysis, testing and drafting. The auditor owns professional judgment and approval.
Do all source documents need to be Markdown?
Native originals do not. AEOS retains and links them as the authoritative evidence. However, information used by AEOS agents and workflows must be available in reviewed Markdown. The user must provide that Markdown working copy, either by converting the information themselves or by using the separate file-converter product.
Does AEOS require one model?
No. It requires an approved provider route and a model capable of the selected workflow.
Can AEOS send audit communication?
No. It may prepare a draft. Only an authorized person can send or publish it.
